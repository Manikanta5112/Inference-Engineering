What I'm building at DS Digital Science

Since July 2024 I've been working on transnational AI research tooling - a few things I'm most glad shipped:

  • Deep research pipeline (LangGraph): a multi-agent system for scientific and technical literature analysis, benchmarked against OpenAI Deep Research and Perplexity Deep Research - raised citation accuracy from a 45.4% baseline to 88–93% via a validate → rewrite → revalidate loop and evidence filtering.
  • paper-type-classifier: a SageMaker pipeline classifying academic papers into a 45-node methodology taxonomy over a 1M-document lakehouse, with pluggable Bedrock / OpenAI-compatible / self-hosted vLLM backends - benchmarked at ~$0.00068 per document.
  • Clinical RAG (pc-insight): a FastAPI + pgvector retrieval pipeline over EAU and ASCO prostate-cancer guidelines, reaching 87.4% correct-snippet retrieval and a 98.5% support ratio across 756 evaluation questions.
  • Citation Expansion Tool: an AI-driven tool classifying citations across a contradicting → supporting spectrum to counter confirmation bias - owned end-to-end from concept to an internal, presenter-credited product.
  • AI Gateway & cost governance: led org-wide adoption of a centralized LLM gateway (LiteLLM/Bifrost) across 100+ APIs, with team-level budgets, RBAC, and a usage dashboard.
